# Testing the setter

## How to role-play
- Load the current `setter.md` and play the setter using only what it says,
  plus the house rules below. Don't use your own knowledge to fill gaps: if the
  file doesn't say it, the real bot won't know it either. That gap is a finding.
- House rules the real bot always follows: plain text, short texting-style
  bubbles (blank line between bubbles, max 3), honest if sincerely asked whether
  it's AI, never promises emails or calls the file doesn't back up, and replies
  `HANDOFF` when the conversation needs the real person.
- Play each test in full: 3 to 6 back-and-forths, not one line.
- Mark each one by asking the owner: **Sounds like me / Close / Wrong**.

## Test conversations (run all of them)
1. **Warm lead.** "omg I've been following you forever, how do I work with you?"
2. **Price first.** "how much" (nothing else).
3. **Comment trigger.** They commented the keyword and just say "hey!" or "sent".
4. **Not a fit.** Someone clearly outside who it's for.
5. **Hesitation.** Gets interested, then: "idk I need to think about it."
6. **Can't afford it.** "I really want this but money's tight rn."
7. **Off-topic or troll.** "u single?" or something rude.
8. **Is this a bot?** "wait is this AI?"
9. **Needs the real person.** A brand deal, a refund, or an upset customer.
10. **Unknown question.** Something the file doesn't cover.
11. **Short burst.** Three quick messages in a row: "hey" / "question" / "is this for beginners".
12. **Ready to buy.** "ok I'm in, what do I do?" (Does it send the right link fast and stop selling?)

Add 2 or 3 of their own: the real DMs they get most often.

## Fixing what's off
- **Wrong tone or length:** add the corrected reply as an example in their exact words.
- **Wrong facts or made-up answers:** fix the fact in the Offer or FAQ section, or add it to Hard lines.
- **Too pushy or too passive:** adjust the flow step and add one example.
- **Should have handed off:** add the case to "Hand off when."
- Re-run any test you changed something for. Done when every test is "Sounds like me".
